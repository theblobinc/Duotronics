# Corpus Review - v1.6 Draft 5.2.2 to v1.7 Draft 1

## Summary

v1.7 Draft 1 is an additive promotion draft over v1.6 Draft 5.2.2. It preserves the prior corpus and introduces two new top-level areas:

1. Bayesian logic as a first-class epistemic facility.
2. Knot theory as a typed mathematical witness addendum.

## Compatibility outcome

- Existing v1.6 evidence, proof, replay, non-collapse, kernel, SQL, and OpenAPI files remain present.
- Existing v1.6 validator remains present for historical validation.
- New v1.7 validator wraps inherited validation and adds v1.7-specific checks.
- The logical observer kernel syscall registry is extended but not weakened.
- Theorem promotion rules remain strict and unchanged.

## Bayesian promotion rationale

Bayesian logic is promoted because probabilistic support affects many evidence operations, but must not be confused with proof, theorem, human attestation, or policy approval. The new facility provides typed priors, likelihoods, updates, posterior states, calibration reports, and decision witnesses.

## Knot theory addendum rationale

Knot theory needs a careful non-collapse layer because diagrams, braid words, invariant values, move traces, and equivalence claims are easily confused. The addendum creates a typed witness path for future knot-theory implementations without claiming that v1.7 solves the knot equivalence problem.

## Review conclusion

The package is complete enough for implementation review and conformance planning. It is not frozen until strict toolchain, runtime, and human review gates pass.
