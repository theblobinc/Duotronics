## Removal and replacement note

This v1.6 draft does not include the older polygon family calculus document as an owning document. Its valid design lessons are absorbed into the general mathematical canon as:

- presence/absence/zero separation;
- declared families and object spaces;
- canonical identity;
- explicit bridges;
- expected-loss declarations;
- replay and fixture requirements.

## Draft truth boundary

Conjectures, theorem-backed facts, computational checks, formal proof artifacts, and analogies are represented with separate statuses.
