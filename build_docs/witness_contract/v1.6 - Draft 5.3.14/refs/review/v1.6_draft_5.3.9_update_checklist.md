- Producer-generated handoff passes the real consumer.
- Inspector imports the generated binding from the sealed handoff.
- Both non-root domains pass real mount-permission tests.
- Inspector build Containerfile builds the active target.
- Both domains have separate verified control evidence.
- Warning-as-error policy is behaviorally enforced.
- One timeout bounds the entire request.
- Lease expiry cannot permit duplicate live execution.
- Cache signing is cryptographically separated.
